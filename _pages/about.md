Hello! I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, working in the **HKUST NLP Group** under the supervision of **Professor Junxian He**. Professor He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.
